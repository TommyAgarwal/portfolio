# Reference 4 — Michelle Liu's Portfolio

## Overview
A visually rich, gradient-forward portfolio with a strong emphasis on beautiful project thumbnails. Blends premium aesthetics with a clean, approachable structure.

---

## Layout & Structure

- **Header/Identity**: Top-left logo (custom red seal/stamp icon) with name "michelle liu" in lowercase below it. Large, clean sans-serif. Below the name, a tagline with emoji: *"Designing useful products to spark moments of delight & human connection. ✨ Previously at 🍎, Roblox, & NASA. 🌿"*
- **Tab Navigation**: Horizontal text tabs directly below the intro: Work · Art · About (with "Work" underlined/active).
- **No traditional nav bar** — the navigation is embedded within the hero content area, not in a separate top bar.
- **Project Grid (Top)**: 2-column grid, 2 rows = 4 large "featured" project cards. These have colored/gradient backgrounds with company logos and imagery overlaid.
- **Project Grid (Bottom)**: 2-column grid, 2 more rows = 4 smaller project cards on neutral gray backgrounds. More subdued.
- **Footer**: Left — logo + name. Center — links (Work, Art, About). Right — "Let's work together!" with email + social icons (Instagram, X, Behance, Pinterest, Apple Music). Below: "Built with Next.js & rosey's mature olives 🫒" — a playful Easter egg.

## Spacing & Proportions

- **Hero/Identity** is compact — ~15% of viewport. Name + tagline + tabs in tight vertical sequence.
- **Featured project cards** are large — each takes up significant viewport area. Aspect ratio ~16:10.
- **Bottom project cards** are smaller, more thumbnail-like.
- **Gutters**: ~16–20px between cards, consistent.
- **Page margins**: Moderate, ~6–8% of viewport width. Content area is wide.
- **Cards fill most of the horizontal space** — this portfolio feels more "full-bleed" compared to the others.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Background | White / very faint warm off-white | Clean base |
| Text Primary | Near-black | Name, descriptions |
| Text Secondary | Medium gray | Tagline, secondary text |
| Logo | Red (custom seal icon) | Distinctive brand mark |
| Featured Card 1 | Iridescent pink-purple-lavender gradient | Apple — premium, dreamy |
| Featured Card 2 | Deep blue / navy with neon accents | Roblox — tech, immersive |
| Featured Card 3 | Warm sunset gradient (orange-pink-purple) | Adobe — creative, vibrant |
| Featured Card 4 | Deep indigo-blue cosmic gradient | NASA — space, technical |
| Bottom Cards | Neutral light gray (`~#EBEBEB`) | Understated, secondary projects |
| Footer Text | Gray | Small, understated |

**Key insight**: The top 4 "hero" project cards use stunning, immersive gradients that reference each company's brand identity. The bottom 4 are neutral gray — creating a clear visual hierarchy between featured and secondary work.

## Typography

- **Name**: Clean sans-serif (likely Inter or system font), ~24–28px, normal weight. Lowercase — "michelle liu".
- **Tagline**: Same sans-serif, ~15–16px, with inline emoji. Casual and friendly.
- **Tab links**: Sans-serif, ~14px, normal weight. Active tab has an underline or bolder weight.
- **Card labels**: Small, bold sans-serif overlaid on thumbnails — "Apple · 2025", "Roblox · 2024" etc. White or dark text depending on background.
- **Footer**: Small sans-serif, ~12–14px.
- **No serif fonts** — entirely sans-serif. Modern and clean.

## Card Design

- **Featured Cards (top 4)**:
  - Large rounded rectangles (~16–20px border-radius)
  - Rich gradient/photographic backgrounds unique to each company
  - Company logos and product imagery composited onto the gradient
  - Small label/badge in the bottom-left corner (e.g., "Apple · 2025")
  - No descriptions below — the visual IS the card
  - Cards are self-contained visual pieces, almost like posters

- **Secondary Cards (bottom 4)**:
  - Same rounded rectangle shape
  - Neutral gray backgrounds
  - Smaller product imagery — phone mockups, screenshots, photo grids
  - Minimal labeling
  - These serve as supplementary work samples

## Unique Design Patterns

1. **Company-branded gradient thumbnails** — Each featured project has a gradient that evokes the company's brand. Apple = iridescent, NASA = cosmic blue, Adobe = creative sunset. Extremely polished.
2. **Two-tier visual hierarchy** — Featured (colorful, large, immersive) vs. secondary (gray, smaller, subdued). Clear importance ranking.
3. **Emoji in tagline** — ✨🍎🌿 in the bio. Adds warmth and personality in a way that's trendy with Gen-Z portfolios.
4. **Lowercase name** — "michelle liu" in all lowercase. Approachable, modern, anti-corporate.
5. **Tab-based navigation** — Work/Art/About as inline tabs rather than a top nav bar. The portfolio feels like a single page with filterable content.
6. **Custom brand mark** — The red seal/stamp logo is distinctive and memorable. Adds a personal brand identity beyond just the name.
7. **Footer Easter egg** — "Built with Next.js & rosey's mature olives 🫒" — personality in the details.
8. **No project descriptions on featured cards** — The visual + company name is enough. Extremely visual-first.

## Overall Vibe
**Premium · Visual · Gradient-rich · Modern · Polished**

This is the most visually striking portfolio of the four. The immersive gradient cards create a gallery-like experience. The combination of lowercase name, emoji, and the red seal logo gives it a unique personal brand. It signals someone who can create beautiful, polished visual work.
