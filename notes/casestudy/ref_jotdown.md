# Case Study Analysis: Jotdown (Kim Rebecca)

**Reference URL:** [kimrebecca.com/jotdown](https://www.kimrebecca.com/jotdown)

## Visual & UI Design

### 1. Layout & Structure
- **Modular Sections:** The case study is broken down into clear, distinct modules (Hero, Problem, Research, Process, Results).
- **Responsive Width:** Content is centered with wide gutters. Images often span wider than the text container for visual rhythm.
- **Narrative Pacing:** Uses a vertical scroll that feels like a story, alternating between dense research and large, high-impact visuals.

### 2. Spacing
- **Extreme Padding:** Clear separation between major sections (e.g., ~100-200px vertical gaps).
- **Consistent Gutters:** Sub-sections and grids within modules maintain a strict horizontal and vertical rhythm.
- **Breathing Room:** Text is never crowded; lines are short and easy to read.

### 3. Visual Elements
- **Device Frames:** UI mockups are always presented in premium device frames (iPhone) with soft shadows and depth.
- **Micro-interactions:** Small graphics or text explain gesture-based features (e.g., "Slide for a new note").
- **Asset Variety:** Mix of screenshots, flow diagrams, and "sketches" to show process.

### 4. Card Design
- **Subtle Containers:** Used for callouts, user quotes, and research stats.
- **Pastel Palette:** Cards often have very light background colors (lavender, soft grey) to differentiate them from the white page background.

### 5. Information Hierarchy
- **Storytelling Headings:** Large, bold serif headings (e.g., "The Problem Space") that tell the story even if you skip the body text.
- **Typography Contrast:** Bold serif headings vs. clean, airy sans-serif body text.
- **Emphasized Stats:** Large, bold numbers (e.g., "7/8 Users") as focal points in research sections.

---

## Content & Storytelling
- **Data-Driven:** Research isn't just mentioned; it's visualized with specific numbers and quotes.
- **Solution-Focused:** Relates every design choice back to a specific user pain point discovered in research.
- **Complete Arc:** Ends with a "Reflections" section and clear links to navigate away or back.
