# Case Study Analysis: Pasteline (Eugene Lazebny)

**Reference URL:** [theeugene.com/project/pasteline](https://theeugene.com/project/pasteline/)

## Visual & UI Design

### 1. Interaction Design
- **Scroll Entry Animations:** Elements fade and slide up slightly as they enter the viewport.
- **Sticky Bottom Progress Nav:** A minimal bar at the bottom (*BASE, RESEARCH, PROTOTYPE, OUTCOME*) highlights based on scroll position.
- **Micro-transitions:** Smooth hover states on buttons and links.

### 2. Use of Color & Accents
- **Canvas:** Neutral white/light-gray background.
- **Section Blocking:** Large sections are contained in very light pastel-colored cards (blue-gray for Problem/Goal, pink for Personas).
- **Brand Highlights:** Vibrant blue used for "eyebrow" labels (e.g., *iOS APP*) and specific highlights.

### 3. Image Presentation
- **Large Corner Radii:** Consistent use of very soft, large rounded corners on all images and cards.
- **Nested Devices:** iPhone and Mac mockups are often grouped inside a containing card to show platform parity.
- **Gallery Grids:** Clean grids for showing design system assets like colors and icons.

### 4. Content Hierarchy
- **Bold Narrative Headings:** Large, clear section titles that anchor the story.
- **Eyebrow Meta-data:** Role, Timeline, and Tools are listed with small caps blue labels.
- **High-Contrast Callouts:** Key statements (Problem statement) are boxed in high-contrast blocks to ensure they aren't missed.

---

## Content & Storytelling
- **Goal-Oriented:** The "Goal" is clearly stated immediately after the "Problem."
- **Platform-Specific:** Highlights designs for both Mobile (iOS) and Desktop (Mac), showing multi-platform thinking.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Story moves from problem definition → research → prototyping → final outcome.
