# Case Study Analysis: User's Figma Draft (Reference 5)

**Reference:** [User's Figma Draft](https://www.figma.com/design/bWXKRXEteH3yLF3912SVH4/Untitled?node-id=1438-883&m=dev)

> [!NOTE]
> The design file was restricted/private during analysis, but the following takeaways are based on the user's explicit instructions and project goals.

## Strategic Direction

### 1. Title Structure: The "Narrative Heading"
- **Descriptive over Functional:** Instead of generic titles like "Process" or "Solution," the headings should tell the story of the project independently.
- **Example Pattern:** "We discovered that users were struggling with X, so we implemented Y to achieve Z."
- **Goal:** A reader should be able to understand the entire case study arc just by reading the headings.

### 2. Information Hierarchy
- **Result-First:** The narrative headings immediately highlight the outcome or the insight, not just the activity.
- **Story-Driven Sections:** The sections are organized to follow a narrative arc of discovery, struggle, and eventual triumph through design.

## Intended Structure (Takeaways)
- Clear, descriptive headings for every section.
- Content-first focus (storytelling over visual polish at this stage).
- Sections that logically lead from problem to validated solution.
