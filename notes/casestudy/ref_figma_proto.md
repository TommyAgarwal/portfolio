# Case Study Analysis: Figma Prototype (User's Favorite)

**Reference:** [Figma Prototype Link](https://www.figma.com/proto/CoWa6UOACPMEQ0s1vPfcEC/UX-Portfolio?page-id=165%3A1159%3Fnode-id=165%3A1159&node-id=521-98190&starting-point-node-id=521%3A95842&t=DDs3J7Jpje9N3cgU-1)

## Visual & UI Design

### 1. Visual Style & Vibe
- **Modern & Card-Based:** Content is encapsulated in large, rounded cards with subtle borders or backgrounds, creating a clear modular feel.
- **Refined Palette:** Uses soft purples, muted blues, and neutral grays. Backgrounds are often slightly off-white (#F5F5F5) to let content cards stand out.
- **Process Transparency:** Includes "behind-the-scenes" visuals like competitive audit spreadsheets and early sketches with handwriting-style annotations.

### 2. Layout & Structure
- **Frame-by-Frame Narrative:**
    - **Context:** Hero image + Role/Time/Tools summary.
    - **Foundation:** Problem statement, Impact/Goals, and Constraints.
    - **Research:** "Discovery Phase" showing personas and survey results.
    - **Execution:** Wireframes and testing iterations.
    - **Validation:** "Before/After" comparisons.
    - **Results:** High-fidelity final features and learnings.

### 3. Interaction Patterns
- **Linear Progression:** Designed for vertical scrolling, but clearly segmented into "chapters."
- **Side-by-Side Comparisons:** Effective use of split-screen layouts to show design evolution.
- **Annotated Mockups:** Direct callouts (arrows/text) pointing to specific UI elements to explain their purpose.

### 4. Use of Visuals
- **Persona Cards:** High-quality photos and detailed bio sections for empathy.
- **Device Frames:** Final UI is shown in high-fidelity iPhone/Android frames.
- **Data Visualization:** Charts and graphs are used to represent research data visually.

---

## Content & Storytelling
- **Methodological Rigor:** Shows exactly *how* decisions were made (not just the final result).
- **Empathy-First:** Significant space dedicated to understanding the user before showing solutions.
- **Clear Value Prop:** Uses bold headings to summarize the impact of each major section.
