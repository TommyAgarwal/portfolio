# Reference 2 — Brandon Lee's Portfolio

## Overview
A bold, confident product designer portfolio with strong typographic presence and subtle use of gradient color on project thumbnails. Clean and structured.

---

## Layout & Structure

- **Navigation**: Centered top bar with evenly spaced links: WORK · ABOUT · [logo] · ANDY · RESUME. The logo acts as the center anchor. All uppercase, small, spaced-out monospaced-style text.
- **Hero Section**: Massive, bold, centered name "BRANDON LEE" in a heavy serif or slab-serif font. Below it, a single centered line in spaced monospace: *"PRODUCT DESIGNER PREVIOUSLY @ APPLE, ROBLOX, SOFI."*
- **No CTA buttons or social links in the hero** — the name and credentials *are* the hero.
- **Project Grid**: 2-column grid, 3 rows = 6 project cards. Each card has a colored thumbnail, title, tags, and a short description.
- **Footer**: Left-side friendly sign-off ("Glad we could cross paths."), right-side columns for CONTACT (Email, LinkedIn, X) and NAVIGATION (Home, About, Resume).

## Spacing & Proportions

- **Hero** occupies ~20% of the page — ample top padding pushing the name down from the nav.
- **Project grid** starts after generous whitespace below the hero.
- **Gutters** between cards: ~20–24px horizontally, ~32–40px vertically.
- **Cards** have generous internal padding — images sit in colored rectangular containers with rounded corners.
- **Page margins**: Wide, likely ~10–12% of viewport width on each side. Content area feels centered and bounded.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Background | Very light gray / off-white (`~#F5F7FA`) | Soft and easy on eyes |
| Text Primary | Near-black | Used for the bold name and project titles |
| Text Secondary | Medium gray | Descriptions and sub-text |
| Card BG 1 | Dark navy/charcoal | SoFi card — dark UI screenshot |
| Card BG 2 | Soft pink gradient (`~#FFD1E8 → #FFB6D9`) | Apple Services — playful pastel |
| Card BG 3 | Light lavender/blue | Roblox Creator Tools |
| Card BG 4 | Vibrant pink-to-teal gradient | Rice Designathon — colorful mobile UI |
| Card BG 5 | Light neutral gray | Arctas |
| Card BG 6 | Warm pink | Sleeve Chrome Extension |
| Tag Pills | Light gray bg, black text | Tiny uppercase tags |

**Key insight**: Each project card has a unique background color/gradient, giving the grid a vibrant, mosaic feel while the surrounding page stays neutral.

## Typography

- **Name**: Ultra-bold serif/slab-serif, massive scale (~80–100px+). All caps. Very high impact.
- **Subtitle**: Monospaced or wide-spaced sans-serif, all caps, small (~12–14px), letter-spacing ~2–4px. Creates contrast with the bold name.
- **Nav links**: Same monospaced/spaced style, all caps, ~11–12px.
- **Project titles**: Bold sans-serif, ~16–18px, left-aligned below thumbnails.
- **Project descriptions**: Regular weight, ~13–14px, gray.
- **Footer heading**: Large, bold serif — "Glad we could cross paths." (~24–28px).
- **Tag pills**: Tiny, uppercase, ~9–10px.

## Card Design

- **Thumbnail**: Large rounded-rectangle containers with distinct background colors/gradients. Images show the actual product — dashboards, app icons, mobile UIs.
- **Title + Tags on same line**: Project title is left-aligned, tags are right-aligned on the same line — a distinctive layout choice.
- **Description**: One line below title/tags.
- **No borders or shadows** — cards are flat, defined by the colored thumbnail container sitting on the neutral page background.
- **Rounded corners**: ~12–16px on thumbnails. Consistent radius.

## Unique Design Patterns

1. **Massive typographic hero** — the name itself is the entire hero. No illustrations, no photos. Pure typography as identity.
2. **Monospaced subtitle** — the all-caps, letter-spaced "PRODUCT DESIGNER PREVIOUSLY @ APPLE, ROBLOX, SOFI" creates a prestigious, matter-of-fact credibility line.
3. **Colorful card backgrounds** — each project thumbnail has a unique background color, creating visual variety in an otherwise minimal layout.
4. **Title + tags on the same row** — an efficient information layout that saves vertical space.
5. **Footer as a friendly send-off** — "Glad we could cross paths" is warm and conversational, contrasting the bold formality of the hero.
6. **Center-anchored navigation** — the logo sits in the middle of the nav, with links on either side. Balanced and symmetrical.

## Overall Vibe
**Bold · Confident · Typographic · Premium · Structured**

This portfolio radiates authority and credentials. The massive serif name paired with the "previously at Apple, Roblox, SoFi" creates immediate credibility. The colorful project thumbnails inject life into an otherwise restrained layout.
