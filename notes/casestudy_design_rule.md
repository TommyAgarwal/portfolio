# Case Study Design Rules

> **The headings tell the story; the work proves it.**

---

## 1. The Narrative Arc (Structure)
Every case study must follow a logical flow that feels like a story, not just a list of tasks. Use the following sequence:

1.  **The Impact (Hero):** A high-fidelity "dream shot" of the final product + a metadata grid (Role, Timeline, Tools).
2.  **The Struggle (Problem):** A descriptive heading defining the user's pain point, supported by research data or quotes.
3.  **The Strategy (Discovery):** Visualizing the bridge between problem and solution (Personas, User Flows, or Audit).
4.  **The Evolution (Process):** Iterative snapshots (sketches/wireframes) with "Before/After" comparisons.
5.  **The Outcome (Solution):** High-fidelity feature deep-dives with annotations explaining "the why."
6.  **The Reflection (Learnings):** What worked, what didn't, and what's next.

---

## 2. Typographic Hierarchy: The Headline Test
Adhere to the "Headline Scan" principle:
- **Headings must be descriptive:** (e.g., Use *"Simplifying complexity for security engineers"* instead of *"Dashboard Design"*).
- **Size Contrast:** Section headers should be dramatically larger and bolder than body text (3-4x).
- **Annotations:** Use a distinct "handwritten" or "technical callout" style for micro-details on mockups.

---

## 3. Visual Layout: The "Breathing Card"
- **Modular Sections:** Group related content (text + image) within distinct modules or subtle cards.
- **Generous Spacing:** Maintain ~120px to ~200px of vertical space between major narrative chapters.
- **Restrained Color:** Keep the page background neutral (white or very light gray). Use color only in the work assets and specific action badges (e.g., "Success", "Danger").

---

## 4. Visual Evidence: Show, Don't Just Tell
- **Device Frames:** Always house final UI inside realistic, high-fidelity device frames (soft shadows, no harsh outlines).
- **Comparison Views:** Use side-by-side "Before/After" layouts to visually validate design improvements.
- **Asset Variety:** Mix polished mockups with "raw" artifacts (sketches, spreadsheets, flow diagrams) to prove rigor.

---

## 5. Metadata & Callouts
- **Metadata Grid:** Use a 4-column grid at the top for project "Stats" (Role, Client, Date, etc.).
- **Bite-Sized Callouts:** User quotes and key stats should live in their own high-contrast components or pastel-colored cards.
