# Reference 3 — Shaahana's Portfolio

## Overview
A minimalist, editorial-style portfolio with maximum whitespace and a small, contained bio card. Emphasizes content breathing room and quiet confidence.

---

## Layout & Structure

- **Navigation**: Top bar with initials/logo ("SV") on the left and right-aligned links: Work · About · Resume. Simple, clean, widely spaced.
- **Hero Section**: Very unusual — a small, outlined card/box (like a sticky note or business card) is positioned slightly off-center, containing the designer's bio. Above the card, a tiny label: "Shaahana's Portfolio". The card reads: *"Shaahana is a technical designer who enjoys solving highly ambiguous problems with a background in Systems Design Engineering from Waterloo."* Below that: *"Previously designed at Coinbase, Meta, and Bubble."*
- **Massive whitespace** above and below the bio card — the hero feels intentionally sparse.
- **Section Title**: "Featured Projects" with a subtitle "Here's some recent design work within mobile and web design." — left-aligned, clean typography.
- **Project Grid**: 2-column grid, 2 rows = 4 projects. Each project is numbered (01–04).
- **Footer**: Simple sign-off: "Thanks for coming by!" with a row of links: LinkedIn · Email · Read.cv · Substack.

## Spacing & Proportions

- **Hero area** takes an unusually large amount of vertical space (~40–50% of initial viewport), mostly whitespace with the small bio card.
- **Bio card** is compact — roughly 300–350px wide, ~150px tall. Sits within the vast white expanse.
- **Project grid** has generous gutters (~24–32px) and ample space between the section header and the first row.
- **Vertical spacing between rows**: ~40–48px.
- **Page margins**: Very wide, ~12–15% of viewport width. The content column feels narrow relative to the page.

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Background | Pure white `#FFFFFF` | Maximum minimalism |
| Text Primary | Near-black / charcoal | Headings and body |
| Text Secondary | Medium gray | Descriptions, sub-labels |
| Bio Card Border | Light gray / thin stroke | Dashed or solid thin outline — subtle |
| Card Thumbnails | Varied — each project has its own color world | Project-specific |
| Accent | None visible | No accent color at all |

**Key insight**: The portfolio is essentially monochrome — black text on white background. Color lives only within the project thumbnails themselves.

## Typography

- **Bio card heading text**: Serif or serif-like font, ~18–20px. Gives an editorial/literary quality.
- **Nav links**: Clean sans-serif, ~14px, normal weight.
- **Section heading ("Featured Projects")**: Large serif, ~28–32px. Elegant, editorial feel.
- **Section subtitle**: Sans-serif, ~14–16px, gray.
- **Project numbers**: Sans-serif, ~14px, light gray ("01", "02", etc.).
- **Project titles**: Italic serif, ~18–20px. The italic creates a sense of motion and elegance.
- **Project descriptions**: Sans-serif, ~13–14px, regular weight, gray.

**Typography is the standout feature** — the mix of serif headings, italic titles, and clean sans-serif body creates a sophisticated editorial voice.

## Card Design

- **Thumbnail**: Large images with subtle rounded corners (~8–12px). Show product screenshots — mobile phone mockups, desktop UIs, component libraries.
- **Numbering**: Each project has a number prefix (01, 02, 03, 04) in light gray — creates order and hierarchy.
- **Title**: Italic serif below the image — distinctive and editorial.
- **Description**: Two lines of sans-serif text.
- **No tags or pills** — much simpler than the other portfolios. Just number + title + description.
- **No borders or shadows** on cards.

## Unique Design Patterns

1. **Small bio card in vast whitespace** — The hero is intentionally understated. The tiny card surrounded by empty space creates a sense of sophistication and anti-design.
2. **No accent color** — Entirely monochromatic outside of project imagery. Bold restraint.
3. **Numbered projects** — "01", "02", "03", "04" gives a curated, editorial feel, like a table of contents.
4. **Italic serif project titles** — Distinctive typographic choice. Adds personality through font style rather than through color or decoration.
5. **Editorial typography** — The serif + italic combination is reminiscent of magazine layouts or literary publications.
6. **"Thanks for coming by!"** — Friendly, casual footer sign-off. Human touch in an otherwise formal layout.
7. **Extreme whitespace ratio** — More whitespace than content. Every element breathes.

## Overall Vibe
**Minimal · Editorial · Sophisticated · Quiet · Confident**

This portfolio says "less is more." The deliberate emptiness suggests that the work speaks for itself. The serif typography gives it an intellectual, editorial quality that stands apart from the typical tech portfolio.
